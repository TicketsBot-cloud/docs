# Premium

The bot is free to use, with three tiers layered on top that raise its limits and unlock extra dashboard features. **Voting Premium** is earned for free by running `/vote` in Discord. **Premium** and **Whitelabel** are subscriptions, available at [tickets.bot/premium](https://tickets.bot/premium) and billed through Patreon.

Whitelabel includes everything in Premium and additionally lets you run the bot under your own name, avatar, and status, so your support system appears as your own bot rather than as Tickets.
